§ 01Our objective

Steady income, not lottery tickets.

Most people leave savings in a bank earning a few percent, or buy stocks and hope they go up. BPSQuant takes a third path: we act like a small, disciplined insurance company for the stock market. Nervous investors pay a premium for protection against big swings — and we collect those premiums using strict mathematical rules.

We don't try to predict the market. We get paid for patience, we cap the risk on every single trade before we enter it, and we aim for small, repeatable wins that compound — not one lucky jackpot.

§ 02The method

We sell insurance — and time pays us.

The moment we take a trade, we collect cash up front — exactly like an insurer collecting a premium. That money lands in the fund before the market moves an inch.

Our positions are balanced, not directional. They're built to profit whether the market drifts up a little, down a little, or sideways — so we never need a crystal ball.

Then time does the quiet work: the protection we sold loses a little value every day it isn't needed. If the market behaves, that premium melts in our favor — and becomes return for you. Deposit, and let the system work.

§ 07Common questions

Questions? Straight answers.

How do I see what's happening with my money?

You get a personal login to the investor portal: your balance, total return, every deposit and withdrawal, every trade the fund makes, and monthly results — updated live.

Can I withdraw my money?

Yes. You request a withdrawal from your portal in one tap; it's reviewed by the manager and paid out at the fund's current unit price — typically within a few business days. No lock-ups.

What are the fees?

2% per year management fee, plus 20% of profits above your account's previous high point (the "high-water mark" — we only earn a performance fee when you make new gains).

Can I lose money?

Yes — all investing carries risk, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we promise is that every trade has a maximum loss fixed before entry, position sizes shrink automatically in stormy markets, and you can see everything we do.

What's the minimum investment?

We're flexible while the fund is growing. Tell us what you're considering in the form below and we'll talk it through.

What exactly do you trade?

Options strategies on three of the world's most liquid ETFs — SPY (S&P 500), QQQ (Nasdaq-100) and DIA (Dow Jones). The full mechanics are explained on our Quant Finance page.
